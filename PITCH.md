# 8-Minute Hackathon Pitch Script

## 0:00 - 1:00 | The Problem
PixelMuse is delightful once, but not sticky.
Users sign up, generate 2-3 images, and leave.
The data says the issue is not onboarding and not output quality.
The issue is no reason to return on Tuesday.
For our target segment, that means lost creator momentum.

## 1:00 - 2:30 | The Insight
Target user: Gen Z social creators (16-24) who post visual content 3-5 times/week.
They do not need more image power. They need structure and social momentum.
Our behavior-change bet: shift from one-off generation to a recurring creation ritual.
So we built Weekly Pose League.

## 2:30 - 5:30 | Live Demo
1. Open app: today has a named challenge theme.
2. Show Pose Coach tips: camera angle, body position, prop use.
3. Generate image in one click.
4. Lock daily check-in and show streak + level progress update.
5. Scroll Remix Reel to show community and personal output in one feed.
6. Click "Jump to next day" and show fresh challenge and continued progression.

## 5:30 - 7:00 | Impact
Primary metric: Day-7 retention.
Baseline: 11%.
Target: 26% for this segment.
Leading indicators:
- Weekly check-ins per activated user (goal: >= 3)
- 7-day streak starts per user
- Share-after-checkin rate

Why this can move retention:
- Time-based pull (new daily challenge)
- Progress loss aversion (streak)
- Identity growth (level + mastery)
- Social curiosity (new remix reel every day)

## 7:00 - 8:00 | What Next
If we had one more week:
1. True Puter.js image generation pipeline with style memory by user.
2. Friend squads and collaborative weekly pose battles.
3. Personalized challenge ranking model based on past completion patterns.

## One-paragraph submission summary
We targeted Gen Z social creators who post several times per week but currently churn from PixelMuse after a novelty session. Our prototype, Weekly Pose League, changes behavior from one-time generation to a recurring ritual by combining daily themed pose challenges, practical pose coaching, streak and level progression, and a remix feed that updates with both community and personal content. We are driving weekly return behavior, measured by Day-7 retention, with a target lift from 11% to 26% in the test cohort, supported by leading indicators including weekly check-ins per activated user and streak-start rate.
